The .hdrbin files in this folder are Radiance RGBE (.hdr) images from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/hdris),
1k resolution, renamed to .hdrbin so the Godot importer skips them (they are decoded at runtime by Cube.Core.IO.RgbeImage).
